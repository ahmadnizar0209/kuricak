System requirements (Windows)

  • Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11, 64-bit (x64)
  • A graphics driver that supports OpenGL 3.3

Kuricak says it could not start

This usually means the graphics driver does not offer OpenGL 3.3. Install the latest driver from your PC or graphics card maker. Some virtual machines and remote desktop sessions do not provide OpenGL 3.3.

Does Kuricak save my text automatically?

No. Kuricak saves a document only when you choose Save (Ctrl+S). Before closing, it asks you to Save, Don't Save or Cancel for every unsaved document. Unsaved text cannot be recovered after a crash or power loss.

Which text encodings are supported?

Kuricak reads UTF-8, UTF-8 with BOM and UTF-16. It saves as UTF-8 or UTF-16 LE, with LF or CRLF line endings. It also reads legacy Windows-1252 and GB18030 text and tells you when it converts it to Unicode on save.

A file changed while it was open

If another program changes a file you have not edited, Kuricak reloads it. If you have unsaved edits, Kuricak asks whether to reload, save a copy, or overwrite.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Ctrl+N new, Ctrl+O open, Ctrl+S save, Ctrl+Shift+S save as
  • Ctrl+F find, Ctrl+H replace, F3 / Shift+F3 next / previous match
  • Ctrl+Tab next document, Ctrl+W close document, Ctrl+, settings

Uninstalling

Uninstall Kuricak from Start or from Settings > Apps. This removes the app and its preferences. Your text files are never touched.

Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · Applies to Kuricak for Windows

Summary

Kuricak does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your documents

The files you open and save stay on your device, in the locations you choose. Kuricak never uploads, copies or sends your text anywhere.

Information stored on your device

To remember your settings, Kuricak keeps a small preferences file on your device only. It contains your appearance choices (such as font, size, colour, theme and transparency), editor options, and the paths of recently opened files. You can clear recent files inside the app, and uninstalling Kuricak removes these preferences. This information never leaves your device.

Purchases

Downloads and any purchases are handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft’s privacy statement. Kuricak does not receive your payment details.

Children

Kuricak collects no information from anyone, including children.
